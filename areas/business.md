# Business & Career

## Purpose

This area covers career growth, value delivery, business context, and strategic contributions in a professional setting.

## Core Focus

- career development
- technical leadership
- strategic impact
- communication and influence
- business alignment
- professional growth and learning
- value creation in a regulated environment

## Goals

- grow as a strong technical leader
- improve alignment between engineering work and business priorities
- communicate clearly with stakeholders and leadership
- build a sustainable career path
- turn technical excellence into business value

## Questions to Revisit

- What work creates the most strategic value?
- How can I influence outcomes without over-engineering?
- What skills do I need to level up next?
- What am I doing that is supporting my long-term career trajectory?

## Systems to Track

- goals and growth reviews
- leadership opportunities
- learning priorities
- stakeholder relationship notes
- career milestones
- role impact and value narratives

## Related Notes

This area connects technical work to the larger business context and personal trajectory.

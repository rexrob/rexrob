# Security & Compliance

## Purpose

This area covers the engineering controls, governance, and standards required in a regulated environment.

## Core Focus

- security engineering
- identity and access management
- secrets and configuration protection
- compliance controls and auditability
- vulnerability management
- change governance and approvals
- secure-by-default architecture patterns

## Goals

- ensure controls are effective and enforceable
- reduce risk without slowing delivery unnecessarily
- improve security visibility and accountability
- support auditability and governance requirements
- build confidence in systems and process maturity

## Questions to Revisit

- What controls are strongest, and where are gaps still present?
- What risks are acceptable, and what needs stricter controls?
- Where do process or tooling create friction or drift?
- How can secure defaults be improved without creating operational drag?

## Systems to Track

- IAM and access reviews
- secrets and key management
- policy and governance reviews
- change and approval workflows
- audit evidence and control tracking
- vulnerability and remediation backlog

## Related Notes

This area is especially important in financial services and regulated environments.

# Platform & Operations

## Purpose

This area focuses on the systems that keep services stable, operable, and resilient in production.

## Core Focus

- platform engineering
- capacity planning and scaling
- service reliability
- incident management and postmortems
- environments and lifecycle management
- operational excellence
- automation and reducing friction

## Goals

- keep systems dependable in production
- reduce time to recover from incidents
- improve platform quality and service health
- create standard operating patterns for engineering teams
- increase confidence across environments and workloads

## Questions to Revisit

- Where is operational risk concentrated?
- What has become too manual or fragile?
- What observability signals are missing or unclear?
- Which service patterns need more resilience or guardrails?

## Systems to Track

- runbooks and SOPs
- service health and alerts
- change management
- reliability reviews
- backup and recovery plans
- environment and dependency tracking

## Related Notes

This is where production operations, reliability improvements, and platform practices belong.
